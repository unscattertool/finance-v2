Each payment to the Tax Commission is tied to the return it paid (same total, or between the return's tax due and its total due). The return's tax due goes to Sales Tax Payable (sales tax return) or Alc Sales Tax (mixed beverage return); interest, penalty and anything paid above them go to Tax Penalties & Interest (an expense). “POS taxable sales” = POS net sales minus alcohol sales while the hotel holds the liquor license (the hotel reports those sales and pays both alcohol taxes); “POS tax collected” = the restaurant's own sales tax. A return above the POS (e.g. June and July) included alcohol sales the hotel also reports.

Gross receipts = what customers paid including tax, before tips. Net sales = after discounts, before tax. Each day comes from the most complete source: Heartland Daily Summary / Taxes reports (from 10/29), the Linga Sale Summary report (before), else Shifts closeouts when every closeout that day has its tax filled in. Alcohol taxes = the 13.5% liquor (mixed beverage) tax — the Heartland Taxes report's liquor tax when imported, else 13.5% of the Daily Summary's alcohol sales (marked est.); for Linga days spread from Linga's liquor tax total — plus, while the hotel holds the liquor license, the sales tax charged on alcohol (Linga days: Linga's Alc Sales Tax in the same proportion; Heartland days: 6.05% of alcohol sales). Those are the hotel's to report and pay, so they are owed to the hotel, not on the restaurant's sales tax return. "Paid next month" = bank payments categorized Sales Tax Payable or Alc Sales Tax dated in the following month. Download CSV gives the daily detail.
