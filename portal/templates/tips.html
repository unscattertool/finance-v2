A deposit that doesn't match means that day's closeouts are missing or incomplete. Sales includes the sales tax customers paid: that part is split off each deposit as tax payable — state sales tax to Sales Tax Payable, mixed beverage tax to Alc Sales Tax — from each business day's card sales × that day's tax ÷ gross receipts (POS day totals). “est.” = not matched to business days yet, or a day without tax figures (estimated from nearby days). Labor, food and beverage cost are what the deposit has to cover (shown here only, not booked): labor = hourly wages for the hours worked on the business days the deposit covers (POS Time Clock, else hours on the Shifts closeouts, × each person's pay rate), in proportion to the deposit's share of each day's sales, + employer payroll tax (from recent Gusto runs); salaries aren't included. Food and beverage cost = the deposit's net sales × what Food COGS and Beverage COGS purchases came to as a share of net sales over the 2 weeks before the deposit. Left = deposit − card tips − sales tax − labor − food and beverage cost. Hover a number for how it was worked out.
